      How these numbers work
      • Data: Open-Meteo Air Quality API (CC BY 4.0, non-commercial use). Values are modelled on a grid, not readings from a monitoring station, and may differ from Malaysia's Department of Environment API (IPU) readings.
      • Scale: US AQI categories, not Malaysia's API scale. Particulate AQI uses a 24-hour average, so it can stay high after the air clears. PM2.5 "now" is the current hourly value.
      • Dose: PM2.5 (µg/m³) × breathing rate × time. Assumed adult breathing: resting 7.5, walking 20, jogging 40, running 60 litres/min. This is air breathed in, not what stays in the lungs.
      • Cigarettes: popular rule of thumb that about 22 µg/m³ of PM2.5 over 24 hours equals one cigarette. It is a rough comparison, not a medical measure.
      • Run verdict: go at PM2.5 ≤ 15 µg/m³ (WHO 24-hour guideline), caution up to 35 (US EPA 24-hour standard), avoid above. Raised one level when the 24-hour US AQI is 151 or higher.
      • Privacy: your location is used only in your browser and sent to Open-Meteo to fetch air quality. Nothing is stored.
